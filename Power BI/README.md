# 🚗 Automotive Insights 2025 | Power BI Dashboard

An interactive **Automotive Sales Analytics Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze vehicle sales, revenue, brands, models, body styles, and vehicle segments.

The dashboard transforms raw automobile sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations, KPIs, and performance analysis.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze automobile sales data and create an interactive dashboard that helps understand:

- Overall vehicle sales performance
- Revenue generated from automobile sales
- Brand-wise sales performance
- Model-wise sales performance
- Best-selling automobile models
- Vehicle segment performance
- Body-style distribution
- Sales contribution of different models

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales by Model
A donut chart showing the contribution of different automobile models to total sales.

### 2. Top Automobile Brands by Sales
A comparison of automobile brands based on total units sold.

### 3. Sales by Body Style
Analyzes sales distribution across different vehicle body styles such as:

- SUV
- Hatchback
- Sedan
- MPV

### 4. Top 10 Best-Selling Models
A ranking visualization highlighting the highest-selling automobile models.

### 5. Vehicle Sales Breakdown
Provides a segment-level view of automobile sales and compares different vehicle categories.

### 6. Detailed Sales Table
The dashboard also contains a detailed table showing:

- Brand
- Model
- Vehicle Segment
- Body Style
- Total Units Sold
- Sales Share %

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard analysis:

- **SUVs** represent the largest vehicle segment in the analyzed dataset.
- **Maruti Suzuki** has a significant contribution among the analyzed brands.
- **Dzire** appears among the leading models by sales.
- The dashboard provides a clear comparison between brands and individual models.
- Sales contribution varies considerably across different automobile models and segments.
